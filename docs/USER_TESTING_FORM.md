# User QA Form

**RPGX Translation Tool — Tell Us How It's Working**

Thanks for using the RPGX Translation Tool! This form is for you to tell us about the quality of the tool — what works well, what's broken, and what feels off. You don't need any technical knowledge to fill it in. Just answer honestly based on your experience.

**This form is anonymous.** Don't enter your name or any personal details — we don't want to know who you are, we just want honest feedback. None of the questions ask for your identity.

**How to fill it in:** For each item, give it a **rating from 1 to 5** based on your experience, and add notes wherever something wasn't right. If you hit a bug, Section 8 has space to describe it.

**Rating scale:**
- **5** — Excellent: worked perfectly, no issues
- **4** — Good: worked well, minor thing off
- **3** — Okay: it worked but felt rough or awkward
- **2** — Poor: mostly didn't work as expected
- **1** — Broken: didn't work or crashed
- **—** — Didn't try this part (skip it)
